 
 COSMOLOGY


Cosmology , the study of the universe on its large scale , encompasses a vast array of topics ranging from the origin and
 evolution of the cosmos to its fundamental structure and eventual fate.

So , in simple words, cosmology is basically the study of the origin , structure and evolution of our universe

Cosmology also studies things like galaxies, star, planets , matter, energy, space, and time.


THE BIG BANG THEORY AND EARLY UNIVERSE


According to this model, the universe began as an immensely hot and dense point around 13.8 billion year ago.

Then it started expanding and even today the universe is continuing to expand.

There are some evidences that support the big bang theory. One important evidence is COSMIC MICROWAVE BACKGROUND 
RADIATION , or CMBR. 

Another evidence is the presence of light elements , mainly HYDROGEN , HELIUM , and LITHIUM, in the amounts predicted by 
the Big Bang Theory
